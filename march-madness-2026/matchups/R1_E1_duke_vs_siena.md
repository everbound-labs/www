### Analysis & Prediction

This matchup presents one of the most lopsided talent differentials in the first round. Duke, the overall No. 1 seed at 32-2, possesses the consensus National Player of the Year in Cameron Boozer, who is averaging a dominant 22.5 points, 10.2 rebounds, and 4.2 assists on elite efficiency (.565 FG%, .409 3PT%). His supporting cast includes a breakout Isaiah Evans and the emerging Cayden Boozer at point guard.

**Why Duke wins convincingly:**

1. **Talent gap is enormous.** Cameron Boozer alone is a matchup nightmare for Siena. At 6-9, 250 pounds with guard skills, there is no one on the Siena roster who can credibly contain him. Duke's roster is littered with future NBA players; Siena has none.

2. **Duke's depth compensates for injuries.** While missing Ngongba and Foster is notable, Duke won the ACC Tournament without them. Maliq Brown (ACC Sixth Man and Defensive Player of the Year) has capably filled in, and the Blue Devils have shown no signs of slipping — they remain unbeaten without these two starters.

3. **Siena's offense may struggle to score.** The Saints averaged just 66.0 PPG allowed on defense, but their own offensive ceiling is limited. Gavin Doty is a legitimate mid-major star at 17.9 PPG, but the drop-off after him and Justice Shoats is significant. Against Duke's length and athleticism, Siena will struggle to find clean looks.

4. **The rebounding and size mismatch.** Even without Ngongba, Duke's frontcourt featuring Cameron Boozer and Maliq Brown will dominate the glass. Siena showed they can rebound (50-26 vs. Merrimack), but the MAAC does not feature the caliber of athletes they will face Thursday.

5. **Historical precedent is overwhelming.** No. 1 seeds are 158-2 all-time against 16-seeds. Duke specifically is 15-0. The two upsets that have occurred (UMBC over Virginia, FDU over Purdue) featured unusual circumstances — Virginia's historically slow tempo was disrupted by UMBC's pace, and FDU caught a Purdue team with severe offensive dysfunction. Duke's balanced attack and elite coaching make them far less susceptible to the kind of stylistic disruption that fuels 16-seed upsets.

**Siena's slim path to competitiveness:**

- If Doty catches fire early and Siena's elite defense can force Duke into a low-possession, grind-it-out game, they could keep it within 15-20 points. Siena's MAAC Tournament showed they can lock in defensively and control tempo.
- If Maliq Brown gets into early foul trouble (as he has recently) and Cayden Boozer struggles from three (sub-29% shooter), Duke could experience some offensive droughts.
- However, even in a "worst case" for Duke, Cameron Boozer's individual brilliance provides an extremely high floor.

**Predicted Score: Duke 82, Siena 56**

Duke's talent, depth, experience under Scheyer, and Cameron Boozer's dominance make this a mismatch of the highest order. The Blue Devils should control this game from start to finish. While Siena's MAAC Tournament run is a great story and Gavin Doty is a player to watch, the Saints are overmatched at every position. Duke advances comfortably and turns its attention to the second round.

**Pick: Duke — Confidence: 97%**

The 3% uncertainty accounts for the razor-thin historical possibility of a 16-over-1 upset (1.25% base rate) and Duke's injury concerns, which very slightly elevate the risk beyond the historical baseline. But this Duke team has already proven it can win big games without Ngongba and Foster, and Cameron Boozer is the type of transcendent talent that makes upsets nearly impossible.
